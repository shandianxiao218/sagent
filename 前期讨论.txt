 B) Full LLM backtesting: Run the entire pipeline (including LLM calls) on historical data. Expensive but most realistic. Could sample key periods rather than running every day.

 C) Paper trading forward: Don't backtest historically at all. Run the system in paper trading mode for 1-3 months to validate with live data before committing real money.

 D) Component-level testing: Test each component separately:
 - Verify 粗筛 filters produce reasonable candidate lists on historical data
 - Verify main line identification on known historical main lines
 - Have LLM analyze known historical setups and compare with what actually happened
